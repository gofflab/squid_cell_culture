**Ciprofloxacin 100x stock**: 1 mg/mL in 0.1N HCl. Filter-sterilize. Store -20C protected from light. Add 1:100 to media.

### 2.2 Enhanced DBSS (Tissue Disinfection)

For the 30-minute tissue disinfection step:
- MBSS base
- Penicillin/Streptomycin: 100 U/mL + 100 ug/mL (2x standard)
- Gentamicin: 50 ug/mL (Thermo Fisher, 15710064)
- Ciprofloxacin: 10 ug/mL
- Amphotericin B: 2.5 ug/mL

### 2.3 Pre-Dissociation Wash

Before dissection:
1. Rinse euthanized animal 3x in sterile FNSW + 100 ug/mL gentamicin.
2. Final rinse 5 min in FNSW + 100 ug/mL gentamicin + 10 ug/mL ciprofloxacin.

### 2.4 Alternative: Primocin

Primocin (InvivoGen, ant-pm-1) at 100 ug/mL can replace the multi-agent cocktail if it proves non-toxic to squid cells. Must be titrated for cytotoxicity before routine use (see Track 1 troubleshooting).

## 3. Euthanasia and Anesthesia

### Standard Protocol (All Experiments)

1. Prepare 7.5% MgCl2 (Fisher Scientific, M33-500) in deionized water (w/v).
2. Mix 1:1 with filtered natural seawater (FNSW). Final MgCl2: 3.75%.
3. Transfer animal to anesthetic solution.
4. Monitor 30 minutes. Confirm: cessation of chromatophore activity, loss of righting reflex, absence of respiratory movements.
5. For euthanasia: confirm death by decapitation or brain destruction.
6. For surgery (resection, transplant): proceed under anesthesia, return to clean FNSW for recovery.

This is the method used in Stock, Hally et al. for all procedures.

## 4. Fixation and Permeabilization

### For Cultured Cells on Glass (Standard)

1. Remove media. Wash 1x with MBSS.
2. Fix: 4% PFA in MBSS, 15 min, 22C. (Prepare fresh from 16% PFA stock, Electron Microscopy Sciences 15714-S.)
3. Wash 3x with MBSS, 5 min each.
4. Permeabilize: 0.5% Triton X-100 (Sigma, T8787) in PBS, 10 min, RT.
5. Wash 2x with PBS.
6. Block (for immunostaining): 4% BSA (MP Biomedicals, 103700) in PBS, 1 hour, RT.

### For Whole-Mount or Thick Tissue (Stock, Hally et al. Protocol)

1. Fix in 4% PFA in FNSW, 4C, 2-5 days.
2. Wash in 1% PBS-T (Tween-20).
3. Transfer to ice-cold acetone, -20C, 2 hours.
4. Wash in 1% PBS-T.
5. Permeabilize in detergent solution: 12 mM sodium deoxycholate (Sigma, D6750), 50 mM Tris pH 7.5 (Thermo, J75825.A1), 1 mM EDTA (Invitrogen, AM9261), 150 mM NaCl (Fisher, S271-3), 1% SDS (BioRad, 1610302) in DEPC-treated H2O.
6. Block: 4% BSA in PBS, 1 hour.

## 5. Antibody Validation Requirements

No immunofluorescence-based conclusion about cell identity may be drawn from an unvalidated antibody. The primary molecular readouts are nucleic acid-based (RT-qPCR, HCR).

### Validation Protocol (Required for Each Antibody)

1. **Bioinformatic**: align immunogen sequence to E. berryi target. >70% identity in epitope region = worth testing.
2. **Western blot**: single band at predicted MW in positive control tissue (white body), absent in negative control (mantle muscle). Multiple bands or unexpected sizes = reject.
3. **Immunofluorescence**: staining pattern on hatchling cryosections must match known mRNA expression from HCR/SOLARmap data.
4. **Controls in every experiment**: secondary-only, isotype control, and internal negative control region.

### High-Probability Cross-Reactive Antibodies

| Target | Clone/Source | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| PCNA | Mouse anti-PCNA, clone PC10 | >85% conserved across metazoans |
| Phospho-histone H3 (Ser10) | Rabbit anti-pH3, Cell Signaling 9701 | Histone H3 essentially identical across eukaryotes |
| Acetylated alpha-tubulin | Mouse clone 6-11B-1, Sigma T7451 | alpha-tubulin extremely conserved |
