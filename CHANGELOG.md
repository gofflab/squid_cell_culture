# Changelog

## v2.0 (Current)

### Major Changes
- Revised entire program in light of Stock, Hally et al. findings on white body as dual neurogenic-hematopoietic organ.
- Track 3 split into Arm 3A (NSC maintenance) and Arm 3B (neurogenic differentiation). Original "neural induction" paradigm replaced with endogenous program-based approach.
- Original Track 5 (Promoter Screening) merged into Track 4 as Phase 3, contingent on DNA delivery.
- Original Track 6 (Proliferation Analysis) renumbered as Track 5.
- Added E. berryi-validated molecular identity panel from SOLARmap spatial transcriptomics data.
- Added DNA fate diagnostic experiment as mandatory first step in Track 4.
- Restructured immortalization strategies with SV40 Large T elevated to co-priority with TERT; mRNA-based delivery prioritized.

### Protocol-Level Changes
- Euthanasia: switched from 5% ethanol to MgCl2 (3.75% in FNSW, 30 min).
- Antimicrobial strategy: added ciprofloxacin (10 ug/mL) and amphotericin B (2.5 ug/mL) to all media; added pre-dissociation gentamicin wash; enhanced DBSS formulation for tissue disinfection.
- All media formulations consolidated in 06_reagents_and_media.md with complete catalog numbers.
- F-ara-EdU parameters anchored to validated in vivo conditions (1 uM, 4h pulse; click chemistry: 2 mM CuSO4, 4 uM azide, 10 mM ascorbate).
- Added osmolality considerations for flow cytometry (MBSS-based buffers).
- Added antibody cross-reactivity validation protocol; shifted primary readouts to RT-qPCR and HCR.
- Animal staging revised: hatchlings (0-7 dph) as primary source for neurogenic work.
- Anterior and posterior white body dissected and cultured separately.

### Risk Assessment Revisions
- Immortalization probability: 70% revised to 30-50%.
- DNA delivery probability: 85% revised to 40-60%.
- Proliferation analysis: >90% (unchanged, now validated by in vivo data).
- Neural differentiation: reframed; Arm 3B (endogenous program) 70-80%.
